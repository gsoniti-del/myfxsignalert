How it reads the market. Each instrument is scored on six weighted factors — higher-timeframe trend, EMA alignment, price vs. trend, MACD momentum, RSI regime, and market structure. A signal only becomes actionable when the trend factor agrees with the net direction and total confluence clears your threshold. Entry is the last close; stop is ATR-based; targets are 1R/2R/3R.
On "accuracy". No indicator predicts price. The only honest measure of a strategy is its historical distribution of outcomes — and even that flatters itself in-sample. The backtest panel splits history into an in-sample 70% and an out-of-sample 30%, and reports a verdict on whether the edge survives on the data the strategy hasn't been "shown" recently. A high full-sample win rate that collapses out-of-sample is a mirage. Past results never guarantee future ones. This tool is for research and education, not investment advice. Trading leveraged instruments like indices, gold and FX carries substantial risk of loss.
